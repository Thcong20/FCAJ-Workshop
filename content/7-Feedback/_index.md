# INTERNSHIP SHARING AND FEEDBACK

### 1. Working Environment
During my internship at FCAJ, I felt the workspace was professional yet open and friendly. Teammates and mentors always supported me, facilitating rapid onboarding.

### 2. Support from Mentors and Admin Team
Mentors guided me step-by-step and answered queries in depth. Instead of handing over answers directly, they guided my search, boosting my analytical thinking.

### 3. Relevance to Academic Major
Assigned tasks were strongly related to cloud computing and software engineering taught at HUTECH, reinforcing my major skills.

### 4. Learning Opportunities
I gained valuable teamwork, communication, and time management skills.

### 5. Team Spirit
I highly value the collaborative spirit of the team. We supported each other through busy sprints.

### 6. Internship Benefits & Policies
The program provided solid support allowances and flexible hours. Workshops and training sessions added great technical value.
